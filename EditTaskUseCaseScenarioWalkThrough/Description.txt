Scenario WalkThrough: The user changes the Completion Progress from 20% to 50%.
- A student opens “CSC207 Assignment” and sees the form prefilled with the task’s current values.
- They change the completion progress from 20% to 50% and click Save.
- The controller sends the form data to the interactor, which finds the task using the student’s ID
  and task ID, validates the input, and saves the update through the DAO. The task keeps its original
  ID and all other field values.
- The presenter updates the view model, and the screen shows 50% progress with a confirmation message.
