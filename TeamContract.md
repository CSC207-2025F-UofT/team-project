# Team Contract

## Purpose of this contract

We agree to share the work fairly, communicate early, and help each other finish a working workout manager that meets the course requirements.

## Team norms and expectations

### Communication

- Use Instagram for team messages and GitHub issues/PRs for tasks, code reviews, and project decisions.
- Reply within 24 hours on weekdays. Flag urgent messages clearly and give advance notice when unavailable.
- Tell the team about blockers, likely delays, absences, or changes that affect someone else's work as soon as possible.
- Give everyone space to contribute. Keep feedback specific to the work and ask for help when needed.

### Attendance and participation

- Attend labs and agreed team meetings on time, ready to contribute. Notify the team beforehand if you cannot attend, or as soon as possible in an emergency.
- Catch up on missed decisions and send your progress update asynchronously.
- Each week, share what you finished, what comes next, and any blockers. Agree on task owners and deadlines together.

### Work quality

- Use branches and pull requests throughout development. Get a review from at least one teammate and address feedback before merging into `main`.
- Test changes before requesting review and add meaningful unit tests for your use case. Run the team's style checks and keep relevant documentation current.
- Follow Clean Architecture and SOLID where practical. Discuss shared interfaces and explain design trade-offs in the PR.
- Prioritize the agreed MVP, including the Swing GUI, external API, and saving/loading data, before adding extras.

## Decision making

Discuss options and try to reach agreement. If we remain split, use a majority vote, with one equal vote per member. Record decisions affecting scope, architecture, or task ownership in GitHub.

## Conflict resolution

Raise concerns directly and respectfully with the people involved. Agree on a concrete next step and a time to check back. If the issue continues or threatens a deadline, ask the tutorial TA or course team for help.

## Accountability

- Each member leads at least one complete MVP use case and makes meaningful code contributions through their own PRs.
- Review teammates' work and offer help without taking over or rewriting their assigned code without discussion.
- Meet agreed deadlines. If a deadline is at risk, notify the team before it passes and agree on a revised plan.
- Everyone helps prepare and rehearse the demo and speaks during the final presentation.
- Give honest peer evaluations based on contributions and these expectations. Raise repeated missed commitments early.

## Signatures

Each member adds their own name after reading and agreeing to this contract. Approve it through a PR with the whole team's agreement. Changes also require everyone's agreement.
