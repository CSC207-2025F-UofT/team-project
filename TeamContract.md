# Team Contract

---
## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

a) Communication

* **Platform:** We will use an Instagram group chat for communication outside of class. Code-related discussion (reviews) will happen on GitHub in pull requests and issues.
* **Response time:** Each teammate agrees to respond to messages within 24 hours.
* **What to notify the team about:**
    * If you think you will not be able to meet a deadline (as early as possible)
    * If you will miss a lecture, lab, or team meeting
    * If you are blocked on your task or need help from another member
    * If you are changing something that affects other people's work
    * If you are unavailable for an extended period (illness, exams, travel)
* **Respectful and inclusive behaviour:**
    * Listen actively to each person's ideas
    * Give feedback that is specific, constructive, and kind
    * Ask questions when something is unclear


b) Attendance & Participation

* Members will attend all scheduled classes and team meetings unless illness or emergencies occur.

* If unable to attend, a member must notify the team in advance and provide input asynchronously.

* During team activities, every member will contribute ideas and engage in the discussion.

c) Decision Making

* Decisions will be made by consensus when possible.

* If consensus cannot be reached, a vote will be taken; majority rules.

d) Work Quality

* All deliverables should be completed on time, tested, and meet the agreed quality standard.

* Members agree to review each other’s work constructively.

* Each Pull Request will be reviewed by at least two team members.

---

### Conflict Resolution
If conflict arises:

The team will first attempt to resolve the issue privately through open discussion.

If the issue persists, the team will involve a member of the course teaching team as mediator.

### Accountability
Persistent non-participation or failure to meet expectations may result in lower peer evaluation scores.
All members agree to provide honest and fair peer evaluations.
