# Team Contract

---
## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* We will be using WhatsApp as our primary method of communication with our team members.

* Each teammate agrees to respond to messages within two days.

* If a member has any scheduling conflicts/obstacles that may interfere with deadlines and lectures, they will notify the rest of the group.

* We will strive to listen to each other's ideas, provide assistance when needed within reasonable bounds, and resolve conflicts in a civil manner through proper communication.

---

## Decision Making

* We will make decisions about our project through a majority vote.

---
## Conflict resolution

* We will resolve conflicts through discussion, taking in all viewpoints and perspectives, calling in other group members if the conflict requires it. We will resort to calling upon a member of the teaching team to act as a mediator only if the situation does not seem to be progressing.

---

## Accountability

* Failure to attend group meetings and expectations may result in lower peer evaluation marks.

* We all agree to provide honest and fair peer evaluations.

---
