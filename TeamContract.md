# Team Contract

---
## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* We will use our Instagram group chat as our primary communication channel.

* Team members will respond to messages within 12 hours on weekdays. 

* Each team member must notify the others if they are unable to meet a deadline.

* All communication will be respectful and constructive. Absences will be notified, and each team member will allow others the opportunity to contribute.  

---

### Work Quality

* All assigned tasks should be completed on time and meet agreed quality standard.

* Each Pull Request will be reviewed by at least two team members.

* Members will give constructive feedback.

---

## Decision Making

* If possible and as much as possible decisions on the whole project should be made unanimously. 

* If a consensus can't be reached, a vote will be taken where majority rules.  

---
## Conflict resolution

* Initially, team members should resolve issues privately through open discussion by listening to each other and comparing perspectives.

* If conflict persists, the team should consult a TA and ask them to mediate the discussion.

---

## Accountability

* Persistent non-participation or failure to meet deadlines may result in lower peer evaluation scores.

* Team members should provide honest peer evaluations.

---

By signing below, we acknowledge that we have read, discussed, and agreed to these terms. We understand that this contract is binding for the duration of the course and may be revised with the agreement of all team members.
