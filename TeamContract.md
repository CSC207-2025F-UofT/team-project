# Team Contract

---
## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* We will communicate outside of class on Discord.
* Each teammate agrees to respond to messages in at most 1 day. Sooner for a time sensitive matter.
* A teammate will notify the team if they aren't meeting deadlines, changes are pushed, or they will be missing a tutorial.
* Respectful and inclusive behaviour are necessary for smooth and productive communication.
Just be nice!
