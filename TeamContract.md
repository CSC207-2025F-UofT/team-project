## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* We will use Discord to communicate about project-related matters.

* Each of us will respond to messages within 12 hours.

* Each teammate should notify the rest when they will miss a lecture or tutorial, if they cannot meet a deadline, or if they wish to change an aspect of the project from how it was last agreed on.

* We will be respectful and inclusive by listening attentively to one another, sharing feedback respectively, and giving each team member a chance to contribute equally.

---

## Decision Making

* How will decisions about your team project be made? (Examples: By majority vote, by unanimous vote, etc.)
* Decisions about the team project will be made by unanimous vote where possible, but if a consensus is not reached within a few days then we will fallback to majority vote.

---
## Conflict Resolution

* We will resolve conflicts by hearing one another out, attempting to understand each others' perspectives, and if all else fails, consulting a neutral third party like a TA or instructor.

---

## Accountability

* Each team member is accountable for the portion of the work they agree to, and for communicating project news and desired changes to other team members.
