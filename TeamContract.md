# Team Contract
## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* We will communicate on discord.

* We will respond to texts within the day. 

* The team notified about: pushes, edits to the code, absences

* We should treat each other with respect. Try to listen to everyone's ideas. Offer help nicely when needed. 

---

## Decision Making

* Give time to everyone to present their ideas. Decide with majority vote.
---
## Conflict resolution

* Listen to both sides and decide on majority vote.

---

## Accountability

* We should all finish work on time. Choose a deadline that can be followed by everyone.
