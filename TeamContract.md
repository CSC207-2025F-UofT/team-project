# Team Contract

## Purpose of this Contract

We want to be a team where everyone feels comfortable sharing ideas, asking for help, and learning from mistakes. This contract outlines what we expect from each other so that we can stay organized, share the workload fairly, and produce work we are proud of.

---

## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

- We will use Discord to communicate outside class and GitHub to track project tasks and review code.
- We will respond to messages within 24 hours on weekdays and 48 hours on weekends. If we need more time to answer, we will let the team know.
- If someone is struggling with a task, might miss a deadline, or cannot attend a meeting, they will tell the team as early as possible so we can adjust together.
- During discussions, we will listen without interrupting and give everyone a chance to contribute before making a decision.
- We will keep feedback focused on the work and explain our suggestions respectfully. Everyone should feel comfortable asking questions.
- We will write down important decisions and task assignments so nobody misses information.

### Attendance and Preparation

- We will attend scheduled meetings and arrive within five minutes of the agreed time.
- If we know we cannot attend, we will give at least 24 hours' notice. For unexpected situations, we will communicate as soon as possible.
- We will complete individual preparation before class so we can contribute to readiness assurance tests and group activities.
- Each person will come to meetings ready to share what they have done, what they plan to do next, and where they need help.
- Anyone who misses a meeting will read the shared notes and confirm their tasks within 24 hours, unless personal circumstances prevent it.

### Sharing the Work

- We will divide tasks based on effort, availability, and what each person wants to learn. We want everyone to contribute fairly and have opportunities to try something new.
- Each task will have a clear owner, deadline, and description of what needs to be completed.
- We will check our workload and progress once a week and redistribute tasks if someone has too much on their plate.
- When possible, we will aim to finish work at least 48 hours before the course deadline so we have time to review and put everything together.
- If a deadline becomes unrealistic, the person responsible will speak up before it passes and work with the team on a revised plan.

### Working on the Project

- We will use separate branches and pull requests for changes to the project.
- Each pull request will explain what changed and how it was tested.
- At least one other teammate will review and approve code before it is merged into main.
- We will respond to assigned reviews within two weekdays or let the author know when we can review their work.
- We will discuss and resolve review feedback before merging. If we disagree with a suggestion, we will explain why and work toward an agreement.
- We will update documentation when our changes affect how others use or work on the project.

---

## Decision Making

We will talk through important decisions together and give everyone a chance to explain their ideas. We will consider the assignment requirements, available time, and effort involved.

We will aim for a decision everyone supports. If we cannot agree within two weekdays, we will use a majority vote of the full team. If the vote is tied, we will revisit the options and ask a TA for guidance if needed.

We will record major decisions and their reasons. Changes to this contract must be agreed to by every team member.

---

## Conflict Resolution

If something is bothering us, we will bring it up early and respectfully. We will describe the specific issue, listen to each person's perspective, and avoid making assumptions about their intentions.

Together, we will agree on what needs to change, who will take action, and when we will check in again. If we cannot resolve the issue after two discussions, or it puts a course deadline at risk, we will ask a TA or instructor for help. Anyone may contact course staff directly about a sensitive or serious concern.

---

## Accountability

We each agree to:

- Complete our assigned work by the agreed deadlines and check that it meets the task requirements.
- Keep our tasks updated and share progress at weekly check-ins.
- Ask for help if we have been stuck for more than one working day.
- Contribute to planning, testing, documentation, and reviews, as well as coding.
- Follow through on commitments and communicate when our availability changes.
- Give fair peer and self-evaluations based on specific examples of communication, preparation, reliability, work quality, and teamwork.

We understand that everyone has other responsibilities and that unexpected situations happen. When someone falls behind, we will first check in and work out what support or adjustments would help. If missed commitments become a pattern, we will agree on specific improvements and a review date. If those improvements do not happen, we will involve the course team.
