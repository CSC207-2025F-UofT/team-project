# Team Contract

---
## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* We will use Instagram to communicate throughout the project.

* Each teammate agrees to respond to messages requested specifically for them or for the entire group in at most 12 hours since the last message.

* We will message if one is unable to meet an internal deadline, if one needs to skip lecture or tutorial and if there is a PR.

* If one were to skip a lecture or tutorial, they will provide input during or after the scheduled time.

* To be respectful, we will make sure everyone has the opportunity to voice their opinions in a safe space with constructive comments and ensure no one feels left behind or unwelcomed by communicating.

---

### Scheduling

* We will agree to find time regularly within a certain period of time to meet and discuss the project.
    

---

## Decision Making

* For major things, listing pros and cons and we do a majority vote.
* For minor things, ask the opinion of at least one other person in the team.

---
## Conflict resolution

* To resolve conflict issues, we will start by attempting to reach a compromise as a team through open and honest communication. If that does not resolve it, we will consult a TA or instructor as a team.)

---

## Accountability

* We will complete our share of the work in a timely manner, keep open communication and seek assistance when needed, and ensure that everyone is on the same page.
